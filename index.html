My approach

My understanding of the current tech world is that there are sufficient tools to do any specialized task that you want to do. You do not necessarily have to be a subject matter expert to get things done. Of course, being an expert gives you an edge over a random person doing it totally with AI, and this leads me to my next point: if you do not get used to these tools that are there to assist you, it will be tough to keep up. A certain level of efficiency is expected from a person in the tech world, and the value of that person is associated with the efficiency with which they operate. Now, with all the tools present in the market, that bar of expectations has risen.

I am also a firm believer in the idea that any hard skill can be learned, or I would say mastered, with a sufficient amount of time and effort. However, soft skills are essential to achieve great things in the long term. In the present scenario, I feel the relevance of soft skills is increasing. Working with AI tools is like managing a small, personal team of highly specialized individuals, and to get the most out of this team, the first thing you have to do is start managing yourself better and more efficiently.
